Privacy policy

How enquiry details are handled.

This page explains what information is collected through the website and how it is used to respond to enquiries.

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Gridkind Studio Privacy Policy

Gridkind Studio collects only the information needed to respond to website enquiries and provide website design, development, hosting, and support services.

Information collected

When you submit the contact form, the website may collect your name, email address, phone number, package interest, preferred call time, and message.

How information is used

Enquiry details are used to reply to your request, recommend a suitable website package, schedule a call, prepare project advice, and provide support if you become a client.

Form processing

The contact form is processed by Web3Forms, which forwards the enquiry details to Gridkind Studio by email. Do not submit passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal records through the form.

Hosting and analytics

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Standard technical logs may be created by hosting providers and network services when visitors access the site.

Sharing information

Enquiry details are not sold. They may be shared only with service providers needed to operate the website, process forms, host the site, or deliver agreed project services.

Contact

To ask about your information or request deletion of an enquiry, email Maxcashgrid@gmail.com.
