Legal terms

Clear terms reduce risk, but they cannot remove every dispute risk.

These terms explain how Gridkind Studio frames website development responsibilities, Australian law references, client obligations, and practical limitations.

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Important legal note

This page is general business information for Gridkind Studio's website and services. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee that a claim, dispute, complaint, or lawsuit can never be made. A qualified Australian lawyer should review any final client contract, proposal, payment terms, refund policy, and liability clause before relying on them commercially. Whether a particular law applies can depend on turnover, business structure, the client, the type of project, and the information being collected or published.

Australian laws and guidance this policy is designed around

  • Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles: relevant when collecting names, emails, phone numbers, enquiry details, and other personal information through website forms. See the OAIC Privacy Act and APP guidance.
  • Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth), Schedule 2 - Australian Consumer Law: relevant to service descriptions, consumer guarantees, refunds, misleading or deceptive conduct, unfair terms, and limitations of liability. Any terms on this site are subject to non-excludable rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
  • Spam Act 2003 (Cth): relevant to commercial email and electronic marketing. Commercial messages generally need consent, accurate sender details, and a functional unsubscribe process.
  • Copyright Act 1968 (Cth): relevant to website copy, images, code, design files, logos, client-supplied assets, stock media, and ownership/licensing of final deliverables.
  • Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth): relevant to electronic communications, online forms, digital acceptance, and electronic project records.
  • Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth) and Australian web accessibility guidance: relevant to making digital services more accessible and reducing discrimination risk. Gridkind aims to use accessible structure, labels, contrast, keyboard access, and responsive layouts where practical.

Website information is not a binding quote

Package descriptions, starting prices, examples, timelines, and website content are general information only. A project starts only after Gridkind Studio and the client agree to a written proposal, quote, statement of work, invoice, or service agreement.

Approvals and project records

Written approvals, change requests, content sign-offs, licence details, and payment records should be kept for each project. Work outside an agreed scope, urgent fixes, launch approvals, and material changes should be confirmed in writing before being treated as approved.

Client responsibilities

Clients are responsible for providing accurate information, reviewing content before launch, confirming that client-supplied text, images, logos, files, and data may lawfully be used, and promptly telling Gridkind Studio about errors, required changes, security concerns, or legal/compliance requirements specific to their industry.

Copyright and project materials

Client-supplied materials remain the client's responsibility. Unless a written agreement says otherwise, reusable methods, templates, processes, general code patterns, and pre-existing Gridkind Studio materials remain available for Gridkind Studio to use in future work. Third-party tools, fonts, images, libraries, plugins, and platforms remain subject to their own licences and terms.

Third-party services

Websites may rely on third-party services such as hosting platforms, domain registrars, DNS providers, email services, form processors, payment providers, analytics tools, booking platforms, and content delivery networks. Gridkind Studio is not responsible for outages, policy changes, pricing changes, account suspensions, delivery failures, or data practices controlled by those providers.

Forms, email, and privacy

Website forms should not be used for passwords, payment card numbers, health information, identity documents, or other sensitive records unless a specific secure workflow has been agreed in writing. Form submissions are handled according to the Privacy Policy.

Consumer guarantees and limitations

Nothing on this site excludes, restricts, or modifies rights that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. To the maximum extent permitted by law, liability for services is limited to re-supplying the affected service, fixing the issue, or refunding the amount paid for the affected service, depending on what the law allows and what is reasonable for the circumstances.

No absolute guarantee

Gridkind Studio aims to build reliable, accessible, and practical websites, but no website can be guaranteed to be uninterrupted, error-free, immune from security threats, permanently compatible with every browser/device, or guaranteed to produce a specific number of leads, sales, rankings, or enquiries.

Governing law

Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, website use and services are governed by the laws of Western Australia and applicable Commonwealth laws of Australia.
