Legal terms

Clear terms reduce risk, but they cannot remove every dispute risk.

These terms are written around Gridkind Studio's actual services: website packages, redesigns, forms, bookings, payments, automation, hosting, support, and launch help.

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Important legal note

This page is general business information for Gridkind Studio's website and services. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee that a claim, dispute, complaint, or lawsuit can never be made. A qualified Australian lawyer should review any final client contract, proposal, payment terms, refund policy, and liability clause before relying on them commercially. Whether a particular law applies can depend on turnover, business structure, the client, the type of project, and the information being collected or published.

Australian laws and guidance this policy is designed around

  • Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles: relevant when Gridkind websites collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, package interest, preferred call times, enquiry notes, support requests, booking details, analytics data, or other personal information.
  • Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth), Schedule 2 - Australian Consumer Law: relevant to Gridkind package descriptions, consumer guarantees for services, service quality, refunds, misleading or deceptive conduct, unfair terms, and liability limits.
  • Spam Act 2003 (Cth): relevant when websites include newsletter signups, email marketing, follow-up automation, CRM workflows, or commercial electronic messages.
  • Copyright Act 1968 (Cth): relevant to website copy, layouts, images, code, design files, logos, client-supplied assets, stock media, icons, fonts, and ownership or licensing of final deliverables.
  • Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth): relevant to electronic communications, website forms, booking requests, digital approvals, emailed quotes, online acceptance, and project records.
  • Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth) and Australian web accessibility guidance: relevant to accessible website structure, form labels, contrast, keyboard access, responsive layouts, and practical steps that reduce accessibility complaint risk.

Free website calls and recommendations

A free website call, review, or package recommendation is general business guidance only. It is not a final quote, legal advice, accounting advice, security audit, marketing guarantee, or agreement to start work. A project starts only when Gridkind Studio and the client agree to a written quote, invoice, proposal, statement of work, or service agreement.

Gridkind Start website package

Gridkind Start is intended for a clean business website presence. Unless the written scope says otherwise, it covers essential website pages, responsive layout, basic contact paths, launch preparation, and agreed hosting setup. It does not include complex booking systems, payments, custom automation, CRM setup, AI chat, large content migrations, regulated-industry compliance review, or ongoing content writing unless those items are expressly included in the written scope.

Gridkind Grow website package

Gridkind Grow is intended for businesses that need stronger enquiry and booking workflows. It may include quote request forms, service filters, booking steps, email notifications, analytics setup, and clearer customer journeys. Clients must confirm the accuracy of service descriptions, booking availability, prices, staff capacity, automated email wording, privacy notices, and any follow-up message consent requirements before launch.

Gridkind Scale website package

Gridkind Scale is intended for payments, automation, AI chat, CRM, newsletters, advanced SEO support, or deeper integrations. These features depend on third-party platforms and client-owned accounts. Clients remain responsible for payment processor approval, product or service fulfilment, refunds, tax settings, chargebacks, fraud controls, customer service, CRM list quality, newsletter consent, and reviewing AI-assisted content or chatbot responses before they are relied on.

Website redesigns and content changes

Redesign work improves structure, clarity, performance, and customer flow based on the agreed scope. It does not guarantee that every historic page, plugin, link, integration, ranking, image, tracking event, or previous website behaviour will be preserved unless that item is listed in the project scope. Clients must identify critical pages, old URLs, required redirects, brand rules, licence limits, and content that must not be changed.

Forms, email notifications, and enquiries

Gridkind can build contact forms, quote forms, booking enquiries, confirmation emails, and form-to-email workflows. Form delivery can be affected by spam filters, email provider rules, incorrect client addresses, third-party form processors, DNS settings, or outages. Clients should test forms before launch and tell Gridkind promptly if enquiries are missing, delayed, duplicated, or going to the wrong inbox. Website forms should not be used for passwords, payment card numbers, health information, identity documents, or other sensitive records unless a specific secure workflow has been agreed in writing.

Bookings, payments, and checkout workflows

Booking flows and payment workflows are set up to match the agreed business rules supplied by the client. Clients are responsible for appointment availability, cancellation rules, refund wording, product or service descriptions, pricing, taxes, payment processor terms, customer disputes, and compliance obligations for the products or services they sell. Gridkind is not a bank, payment processor, accountant, tax adviser, or financial services provider.

Automation, CRM, newsletters, and AI chat

Automation can help route enquiries, send notifications, update lists, or trigger follow-up steps. Clients must approve the workflow, message wording, recipient lists, consent settings, and business rules before launch. AI chat and automated responses are support tools only and may be incomplete or wrong. Clients must review and maintain automated content, especially where pricing, availability, legal obligations, health, finance, guarantees, or regulated services are involved.

Hosting, domains, DNS, and support

Monthly hosting and support is intended to cover agreed hosting, uptime support, basic maintenance, security monitoring, minor updates, troubleshooting, and practical help. It does not include unlimited redesigns, major new features, third-party subscription fees, domain registration fees, paid plugins, emergency work caused by client account loss, or outages controlled by registrars, hosting platforms, email providers, payment processors, or DNS networks. Clients are responsible for keeping domain ownership, registrar access, billing details, and account recovery details current.

SEO, analytics, speed, and business results

Gridkind can improve technical SEO basics, headings, metadata, content structure, page clarity, analytics setup, and performance decisions within the agreed scope. Search rankings, traffic, leads, bookings, sales, conversion rates, advertising outcomes, and revenue depend on many factors outside Gridkind's control and are not guaranteed.

Acceptable use and refused work

Gridkind may refuse, pause, remove, or stop work on websites, content, integrations, automations, or support requests that appear to involve unlawful conduct, scams, phishing, malware, spam, misleading claims, impersonation, counterfeit or stolen goods, copyright infringement, privacy breaches, harassment, discriminatory material, unsafe regulated claims, or activity that may breach a third-party platform's policies. Clients are responsible for making sure their business, offers, claims, content, customer lists, and requested workflows are lawful and suitable for publication.

Payments, cancellations, and suspended services

Deposits, staged payments, monthly hosting/support fees, third-party subscriptions, domain fees, paid plugins, and out-of-scope work are handled according to the agreed quote, invoice, or service agreement. If invoices are overdue, Gridkind may pause new work, delay launch, suspend non-essential support, or stop paid hosting/support services after reasonable notice where the law allows. Cancellations may still require payment for work already completed, committed third-party costs, and approved project time.

Client accounts, passwords, and access security

Clients are responsible for keeping domain registrar, hosting, email, payment processor, CRM, analytics, booking, advertising, and social media accounts secure. This includes using strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, accurate recovery details, and correct billing information. Where possible, access should be provided by secure account invitation instead of sharing passwords. Gridkind is not responsible for losses caused by compromised client accounts, expired payment cards, lost recovery access, or unauthorised changes made by the client or their other providers.

Cookies, pixels, analytics, and tracking tools

Analytics, advertising pixels, retargeting tags, heatmaps, CRM tracking, newsletter tracking, and similar tools should only be added after the client approves their use. Clients are responsible for confirming whether their website needs cookie notices, consent banners, privacy disclosures, opt-outs, or platform-specific notices based on their customers, marketing activity, target locations, and applicable law.

Client customer data and processing

When Gridkind handles enquiry data, booking details, support requests, CRM lists, form submissions, or analytics information for a client project, that information is used to deliver the agreed service, troubleshoot the website, provide support, or complete approved workflow tasks. The client remains responsible for the privacy obligations owed to their own customers, including collection notices, consent, retention, access requests, correction requests, deletion requests, and lawful use of customer lists.

Approvals and project records

Written approvals, change requests, content sign-offs, licence details, and payment records should be kept for each project. Work outside an agreed scope, urgent fixes, launch approvals, and material changes should be confirmed in writing before being treated as approved.

Client responsibilities

Clients are responsible for providing accurate information, reviewing content before launch, checking package inclusions, testing forms and workflows, confirming that client-supplied text, images, logos, files, product details, prices, claims, testimonials, and data may lawfully be used, and promptly telling Gridkind Studio about errors, required changes, security concerns, accessibility needs, or legal/compliance requirements specific to their industry.

Copyright and project materials

Client-supplied materials remain the client's responsibility. Unless a written agreement says otherwise, reusable methods, templates, processes, general code patterns, and pre-existing Gridkind Studio materials remain available for Gridkind Studio to use in future work. Third-party tools, fonts, images, libraries, plugins, and platforms remain subject to their own licences and terms.

Final ownership and portfolio use

After full payment, the client may use the final approved client-specific website content, page layouts, and delivered assets for their own business website, subject to any third-party licence limits and the written project scope. Gridkind retains ownership of pre-existing materials, reusable processes, general know-how, reusable code patterns, templates, and internal tools. Unless a written agreement says otherwise, Gridkind may mention the completed work in its portfolio, proposals, and marketing materials.

Third-party services

Websites may rely on third-party services such as hosting platforms, domain registrars, DNS providers, email services, form processors, payment providers, analytics tools, booking platforms, and content delivery networks. Gridkind Studio is not responsible for outages, policy changes, pricing changes, account suspensions, delivery failures, or data practices controlled by those providers.

Privacy and marketing compliance

Form submissions and website enquiries are handled according to the Privacy Policy. If a client asks Gridkind to add newsletters, CRM lists, retargeting, analytics, marketing automation, or follow-up email sequences, the client must confirm that collection notices, consent records, unsubscribe processes, sender details, and list sources are suitable for their business and comply with applicable law.

Consumer guarantees and limitations

Nothing on this site excludes, restricts, or modifies rights that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. To the maximum extent permitted by law, liability for services is limited to re-supplying the affected service, fixing the issue, or refunding the amount paid for the affected service, depending on what the law allows and what is reasonable for the circumstances.

No absolute guarantee

Gridkind Studio aims to build reliable, accessible, and practical websites, but no website can be guaranteed to be uninterrupted, error-free, immune from security threats, permanently compatible with every browser/device, or guaranteed to produce a specific number of leads, sales, rankings, or enquiries.

Backups, maintenance, and security

Gridkind aims to take reasonable steps for secure builds, updates, monitoring, backups, and maintenance within the agreed support scope. No website or hosting setup can be guaranteed to be immune from hacking, data loss, downtime, spam, provider outages, browser changes, platform changes, or human error. Clients should keep their own copies of important business records, customer records, product information, login recovery details, and legal documents.

Governing law

Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, website use and services are governed by the laws of Western Australia and applicable Commonwealth laws of Australia.

Dispute resolution

If a dispute arises, the client and Gridkind should first try to resolve it by written notice, clear details of the issue, and a reasonable opportunity to fix or respond. Where practical, the parties should consider negotiation or mediation before starting court proceedings, except where urgent action is needed to protect rights, payment, security, confidential information, or intellectual property.

Changes to these terms

Gridkind may update these website legal terms as services, tools, laws, platform requirements, or business processes change. The terms published at the time a client accepts a written quote, invoice, proposal, statement of work, or service agreement should be read together with that written agreement.
