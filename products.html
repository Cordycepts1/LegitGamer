Upgrade path

Start focused, then add capability when it earns its place.

A small business website should be able to grow without a rebuild every time the business adds a new service, location, booking need, or payment model.

  1. Launch the core site

    Get the offer, pages, calls to action, hosting, and contact path working cleanly.

  2. Improve the enquiry flow

    Add quote forms, service filters, booking steps, and analytics once traffic has a pattern.

  3. Connect business systems

    Introduce payments, automation, email routing, CRM, newsletter, or AI chat when the workflow needs it.
