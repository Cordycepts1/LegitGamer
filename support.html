Support FAQ

Questions after launch.

How do I request a small update?

Use the contact form and include the page, the exact text or image change, and any deadline. Minor updates are part of monthly support.

What if my form stops sending enquiries?

Send a support request with the page URL and a short description of what happened. We can check form settings, notifications, spam filtering, and routing.

Can support include new features?

Small adjustments are covered. New payments, ecommerce, CRM, newsletter, AI chat, or automation work is scoped as an upgrade so the work is clear.

Can the site keep improving after launch?

Yes. Support can turn live customer behaviour into better page structure, clearer calls to action, and new features when the business is ready.
